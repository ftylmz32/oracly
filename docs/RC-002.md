# RC-002 — AI Conversation Experience

**Status:** Canonical · Calm reflective companion — not a chatbot  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Complete AI chat experience (tab chat + post-reading oracle)  
**Hierarchy:** [Master Directive](./MASTER_DIRECTIVE.md) → … → [RC-001 Reveal & Reading](./RC-001.md) → **RC-002 AI Conversation** → [RC-003 Resilient Experience](./RC-003.md) → [AI Agent Guide](./AI_AGENT_GUIDE.md)

---

> *Users should leave thinking: "That conversation helped me reflect."*

Do **not** redesign Home. Do **not** change navigation. Perfect the existing experience only.

---

## Mission

Transform AI chat into a calm, premium, thoughtful conversation with a quiet reflective companion.

---

## First impression

- Profile-aware welcome — never hardcoded names or generic chatbot openers
- Oracle empty state invites reflection, not commands
- Subtitle: "Sakin bir yansıma alanı" — not "● Online"

---

## Conversation tone

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Warm, concise, balanced | Long walls of text |
| Short paragraphs | Fortune-telling certainty |
| Occasional reflective questions | Constant answers |
| Real memory only | Invented memories |
| Gentle closing | Pressure to continue |

---

## Visual experience

- Messages breathe — wider spacing, 1.65 line height
- Smooth entrance animation (no layout jump)
- Natural scroll with `easeOutCubic`
- Reduced visual clutter (no duplicate typing animations, fewer particles)

---

## Ending

- `ConversationClosingWhisper` after meaningful exchange
- Permission to leave peacefully — no "keep chatting" pressure

---
