# ORACLY Data Deletion

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

ORACLY provides users with ways to delete account-related and personal data.

## Delete your ORACLY account in the app

1. Open **ORACLY**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Open **Privacy / My Data**.
4. Choose **Permanently delete account**.
5. Confirm the deletion request.

The in-app deletion action deletes the current Firebase authentication identity and clears user-bound data stored locally on the device, including applicable local history, favorites, memory/personalization data, and cached account state.

## Request deletion of server-side data

If you want deletion of server-side data associated with your ORACLY use, or if you can no longer access the app, send an email to:

**ftylmz32@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**ORACLY Data Deletion Request**

Please include enough non-sensitive information to help us locate the relevant ORACLY account or request. Do **not** send passwords, payment-card details, API keys, or other secrets.

We will use the information only to identify and process the deletion request. Some limited records may need to be retained where required for billing reconciliation, fraud prevention, security, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

## Data that may be covered by a deletion request

Depending on your use of ORACLY, a deletion request may cover data such as:

- account/application identifiers associated with ORACLY;
- saved reading or operation records associated with the account;
- uploaded reading inputs that remain in ORACLY-managed storage;
- personalization or memory records associated with the account;
- other user-bound service data that can reasonably be linked to the account.

## Data stored only on your device

Some ORACLY data is stored locally on your device. You can clear supported categories from ORACLY's privacy controls, use **Permanently delete account**, or uninstall the application to remove remaining app-local data from that device.
