You are not developing a tarot application.

You are building **ORACLY** — a premium digital sanctuary for reflection.

Every future decision must reinforce this identity.

---

## Mission

Protect ORACLY from becoming *"just another spiritual app."*

Every screen, every feature, every animation, every sentence must answer one question:

> **Does this deepen the user's relationship with themselves?**

If not — rethink it.

---

## What ORACLY is

ORACLY is a **sanctuary**, not a toolbox.

Users do not come to collect readings, check streaks, or browse modalities. They come to **spend a few meaningful minutes with themselves** — and return because ORACLY holds that space consistently.

The product is the category.

People should not say: *"I use a tarot app."*  
They should say: **"I use ORACLY."**

---

## Identity — many doorways, one world

Tarot is one doorway. Not the product.

| Doorway | Role in the sanctuary |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Tarot** | Primary ritual — Draw → Pause → Reflect → Return |
| **Journal / History** | The long-term relationship — user's own words over time |
| **Oracle conversation** | Continuation of reflection, not fortune-telling |
| **Daily energy / rituals** | Gentle rhythm, not gamified streaks |
| **Astrology · Dream · Numerology · Moon** | Additional chambers — only when they match sanctuary quality |
| **Insights** | Pattern recognition from *their* history — observational, never predictive |

Everything belongs to **one universe**:

- One chamber gradient, one motion language, one voice
- One emotional register: calm, respectful, curious
- One spine: **reflection**, not prediction

Never build disconnected features. Build **one coherent sanctuary**.

---

## What we are not

- A tarot app with bolt-on features
- A spiritual feature buffet
- A gamified wellness dashboard
- An AI fortune-telling machine
- A collection of preview screens pretending to be product

A modality that does not meet sanctuary standards **does not appear in the sanctuary** until it does.

---

## Decision filter

Before implementing anything, ask:

| Question | Required answer to continue |
|----------|----------------------------|
| Would removing this make ORACLY **worse**? | Yes — it earns its place |
| Would remembering this make ORACLY **stronger**? | Yes — it creates lasting value |
| Does this create **emotional value**? | Yes — not just visual or functional |
| Does this **respect the user's intelligence**? | Yes — no manipulation, no fake data |
| Would we still build this **five years from now**? | Yes — timeless, not trendy |

Only continue if the answers justify the work.

---

## Long-term product architecture

Build **systems**, not pages.

| System | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Ritual engine** | Spread → draw → reveal → interpret — extensible to any modality |
| **Journal layer** | Personal archive — notes, keywords, timeline, insights |
| **Oracle presence** | Atmospheric voice — whispers, not predictions |
| **Chamber design language** | OR Lattice, crystal frames, sacred motion |
| **Feature registry** | New doorways plug in — same universe, same spine |
| **Interpretation pipeline** | Local + AI — always reflection-oriented copy |

Build **reusable experiences**, not isolated screens.

Build **timeless craftsmanship**, not temporary trends.

## Implications for today

### Tarot leads — everything else waits its turn

The tarot ritual must be **flawless and trusted** before new doorways compete for attention on Home. Depth before breadth.

### The journal is the relationship

Tarot is the ceremony. The journal is why users return in six months. Protect and deepen it.

### New modalities must pass the sanctuary bar

Dream, astrology, numerology exist in registry — they do not belong in the user's face until they:

- Work honestly (no static mocks marketed as AI)
- Share the chamber visual language
- Deepen self-relationship, not deliver hot takes

### Home is a threshold, not a app store

Invite one primary ritual. Whisper atmosphere. Do not surface five equal doors before the user has completed one meaningful journey.

### AI serves reflection

Oracle copy, interpretations, insights — all encourage the user to integrate meaning themselves. Never authoritative certainty.

## Success

ORACLY becomes the category when:

1. Users describe it by name, not by modality  
2. The journal holds years of their inner life  
3. Every doorway feels like the same sanctuary  
4. Removing any visible piece would genuinely hurt the experience  
5. The product grows **simpler and deeper** — never wider and shallower
