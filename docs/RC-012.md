## Mission

Help first-time users immediately understand what ORACLY is, what it is not, and why it is different — without long explanations.

---

## Onboarding (3 slides)

| Slide | Message |
|-------|---------|
| ORACLY | Sanctuary for pause and reflection |
| Nasıl çalışır? | Card → breath → read — no pressure to find answers |
| Ne değildir? | Not fortune-telling; interpretations are invitations |

**CTA:** `İlk kartını çek` → Tarot tab + Tek Kart flow (via `FirstSessionIntent`)

No premium slide before first value.
