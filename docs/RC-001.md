---

## Reveal

- Ceremonial pause before flip — not slow, not game-like
- Card art precached during sacred moment
- Safe-area aware result panel
- Error recovery when session is missing

---

## Reading hierarchy

The eye follows:

1. Card identity (header)
2. Core reflection (Genel Anlam — full text, emphasized)
3. Deeper interpretation (domain sections)
4. Reflection questions (Ruhsal Rehberlik — full text)
5. Gentle suggestion + closing blocks (EPIC-015)
6. Transparency footnote (EPIC-014)

Duplicate tagline sections are skipped when they repeat the header.

---

## Readability

- Wider line height and section spacing (`ReadingSacredRhythm`)
- Core and reflective sections preserve full text (no aggressive condense)
- Intro shows breath copy — not duplicate card name/tagline

---

## Performance

- Ambient loop isolated from content rebuilds on reading screen
- Card image precached before reveal flip
