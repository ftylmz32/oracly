## Mission

Review every visible detail.

**Do not redesign. Do not add features. Do not increase complexity.**

Ask repeatedly:

> *What still reminds the user that this is software?*

Reduce that feeling wherever possible.

Replace anything that feels:

| Digital | Crafted |
|---------|---------|
| Mechanical | Weighted |
| Repetitive | Varied |
| Perfectly symmetrical without purpose | Intentionally composed |
| Abruptly animated | Naturally finished |
| Computer-generated | Hand-tuned |
| Generic | Sanctuary-specific |

## Human craft

- Every surface **intentionally made**
- Every transition **naturally finished**
- Every pause **deliberate**
- Every movement respects **gravity and weight**
- Nothing **synthetic**

---

## Emotional continuity

The user should never mentally leave the ORACLY universe.

- Every screen inherits atmosphere from the previous one
- Nothing resets
- Nothing breaks immersion

---

## Micro imperfection

Real handcrafted objects are never mathematically perfect.

Where appropriate, introduce **tiny natural variations** — almost invisible:

- Stagger jitter (prime offsets, not arithmetic steps)
- Desynchronized breath phases per grid index
- Incommensurate pulse periods (12s–23s, not 700ms badges)
- Chamber waiting orb instead of Material spinners
- Tab crossfade instead of hard cuts
