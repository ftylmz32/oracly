## Stores

| Store | Content | Owner key |
|---|---|---|
| `speechGrants/{grantId}` | `identity`, `kind`, reply `text` (≤1200 chars), `expiresAtMs` (playback end), `expiresAt` (TTL), claim fields, `spent`, `clips` (signature → object path) | `identity` (= `sub:`+sha256(UID)[:24]) |
| `speechPreviewDay/{sha256(identity\|day)}` | `identity`, `spent`, `clips`, claim fields, `expiresAt` (TTL) — the free preview cap (12 settings / UTC day) | `identity` |

text (`ai/openai-speech.ts`); the user's voice is never stored.

|---|---|---|

| Account deletion | Speech records and clips **not** deleted | `speechGrants` + `speechPreviewDay` (by `identity`) and every object under `speech-clips/<owner>/` (incl. orphans) deleted; objects first, records second; any failure → receipt stays `deleting`, retry resumes |

### Ordering (TTL record vs MP3)

Clips are never deleted *through* their record. The record expires at the
playback end (6 h); the clip by object age (2 days):

- record deleted first (the normal case) → the clip has no reference, the
  age rule removes it; nothing can be stranded forever;
- clip deleted first (TTL lag > 2 days) → the record no longer grants
  playback (its `expiresAtMs` passed), so a missing clip is never needed;
- preview: one record per UTC day; its clips are read only that day
  (< 24 h); the record is kept until the 2nd day after.

Firestore TTL deletes **after** `expiresAt`, typically within 24 hours, with
no upper guarantee. GCS lifecycle actions are asynchronous as well (usually
within a day of meeting the condition). Real retention is therefore
"at least until the instant, usually removed within about a day after it".

## Real retention (after the cloud steps are applied)

| Data | Earliest removal | Expected removal |
|---|---|---|
| Grant record (incl. reply text) | 6 h after issue | ~6 h + up to ~24 h TTL lag |
| Preview-day record | start of day+2 (UTC) | + up to ~24 h TTL lag |
| MP3 clip | 2 days after creation | + up to ~24 h lifecycle lag |
| Orphan clip from a failed finish | immediately (request-time delete) | otherwise as MP3 |
| All of the above on account deletion | at deletion | at deletion (synchronous) |

Without the cloud steps only account deletion and the request-time orphan
delete are in effect; everything else stays until the cleanup tool runs.

## Historical data — dry run first

`scripts/speech-clips-cleanup.ts`:

1. **Inventory (read-only, default).** Reads `speechGrants` and
   `speechPreviewDay`, then lists `speech-clips/` only. Prints counts per
   decision and records without `expiresAt`; prints no identity, text or
   owner segment.
2. **Criteria.** Only names matching
   `speech-clips/<24 hex>/<1–32 hex>/<20 hex>.mp3` or
   `speech-clips/<24 hex>/_probe/ready.txt` are considered.
   - `expired`: object age ≥ 2 days;
   - `orphan`: no record's `clips` points at it and age ≥ 1 h (finish runs
     within seconds; claims last 70 s);
   - `stale_probe`: probe older than 1 h;
   - kept: referenced, or younger than 1 h; skipped: wrong shape or no
     creation time.
   Records without `expiresAt`: grants get their `expiresAtMs`, preview days
   two days from now (their day is not stored; this cannot reset a cap).
3. **Safety.** Other prefixes are never listed; every name is re-validated
   at apply time; the plan is refused above `--max-deletes` (default 500);
   apply needs `--apply`, `--confirm-bucket=<exact bucket>` and
   `ORACLY_SPEECH_CLEANUP_APPROVED=yes`; backfill never moves an existing
   `expiresAt` and never re-creates a deleted record; deletes are
   `ignoreNotFound`.
4. **Apply** only after the PO reviews the dry-run counts.
