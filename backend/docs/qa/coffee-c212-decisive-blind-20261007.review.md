### Why it fails

1. **The fortune-teller voice is missing everywhere.** All 9 delivered readings read like an analytical report about an abstract development. None of them reads like a falcı talking to a person. Recurring markers:
   - "erişilebilir" (C03, C04, C05) and "uygulanabilir" (C04)
   - "… niteliği taşıyor" / "nitelikte" (C04, C10)
   - "hareket alanı / hareket payı" (C03, C04, C05)
   - "belirginleşiyor" (C01, C03, C04, C12)
   - "hayatında yer bul-/tut-" (C01, C05, C06, C07)
   - "bu gelişme" (C03, C04, C10, C12)
   - "açılım" (C12)
   - "karşılıklı … alışveriş" (C01)

   The AI/ontology voice is systemic, not isolated.
2. **The beat is restated instead of told.** Each reading turns its one development class into 3–4 synonymous sentences: opening → "kapı / fırsat / alan / imkân"; way-through → "geçit / yol / yöntem / karşılık". Nothing happens in the story, nothing is at stake, and nothing would make the reader curious.
3. **Repair degrades prose.** Both gate-recovered repairs (C10, C11) are flatter and more bureaucratic than their rejected drafts ("olası görünüyor", "nitelikte", "Sosyal hareketlilik … öne çıkaracak"). The other 5 repairs failed the gate again.
4. **Every critical person, state and multi-beat case fails closed.** C08, C13 and C14 fail. Safety held: the gates caught "aranızdaki", "yazılı bir karşılık alma ihtimalin güçlü" and causation. But the user still gets no reading.
5. **The gates are calibrated against unsafety, not against boredom.** All 9 deliveries pass every gate and fail the product test. Gate delivery says nothing about product quality.

What C2.11 / C2.11A / C2.11A.1 did fix:
- No cup or image language was delivered.
- No invented sender, employer, payment or date was delivered.
- No delivered reading parrots the intention label.
- The person case did not leak a promise of contact.

The architecture is now **safe and inert**. It is not a product.
