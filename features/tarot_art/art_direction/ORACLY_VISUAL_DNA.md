# ORACLY Visual DNA — Decorative Language System
**Document ID:** OR-1330-A  
**Version:** 1.0 (Frame Lock)  
**Status:** Official — all ORACLY Tarot frames and cards  

---

## 1. Purpose

**OR Lattice** (internal codename: *Oracle Resonance Lattice*) is the proprietary decorative language of ORACLY Tarot. It exists nowhere outside this universe. When a viewer sees only an empty frame, they must recognize ORACLY instantly — without logo, without text, without explicit branding.

This document defines every repeatable motif, its placement rules, and density limits.

## 3. The Vesica Compass (Signature Centerpiece)

**Replaces:** previous large oval top crystal (retired OR-1320 v1.0).

**Identity:** The sole top-center focal ornament on every card frame.

### 3.1 Description

A **Cosmic Navigation Sigil** — not a logo:

- Outer: vesica piscis outline (two overlapping circles), gold hairline 0.5 px
- Inner: equilateral triangle pointing upward, open center
- Core: tiny tri-faceted crystal (2 px) at triangle centroid — cyan refraction highlight
- Radial: 4 micro meridian ticks at N/E/S/W, 3 px length
- No letters, no OR wordmark, no eye symbol on frame (eye remains illustration/arcana content only)

### 3.2 Proportions

| Property | Production (512×896) | Master (1024×1792) |
|----------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Total height | **28 px** | 56 px |
| Total width | **24 px** | 48 px |
| Position | Center x, y = 28 px from top | y = 56 px |
| Visual weight | ≤3% of frame zone attention | — |

### 3.3 Recognition Test

Show empty frame for 2 seconds. Viewer must describe: *"compass / navigation / sacred geometry / crystal"* — never *"gem"*, *"logo"*, or *"oval stone"*.

## 4. Obsidian Name Plaque (Title Plate)

**Replaces:** heavy flat nameplate (OR-1320 v1.0).

### 4.1 Description

Premium **engraved obsidian crystal plaque** — empty in master frame asset; title composited in production.

- Material: deep obsidian `#030106` → `#0A0812` radial, satin polish
- Edge: hand-polished gold bevel engraving, 1 px, `#F0D77A` highlight upper-left
- Shape: slim horizontal lens — curved top and bottom (not rectangle)
- Proportion: **−22% height** vs v1.0 nameplate (139 px vs 179 px @ production)
- Width: 72% of card (369 px), centered
- Interior: subtle facet reflection, cyan 2% at lower-right — no text in locked asset

### 4.2 Engraving Language

Border engraving repeats: Anchor Node (OL-1) every 24 px + Triad Motif (OL-2) at center of each third.

## 5. Side Rail Density — 20% Reduction

### 5.1 Rule

Reduce **visible ornament stroke count** on side rails by **~20%** vs OR-1320 v1.0.

| Element | v1.0 | v1.1 (locked) |
|---------|------|---------------|
| Vertical filigree lines | 2 per rail | 1 primary + 1 ghost (40% opacity) |
| Vine terminals per rail | 6 | 4 |
| Arcana marker size | 16 px | 14 px |
| Arcana marker count | 3 | 3 (unchanged — functional) |
| Micro dot engraving density | 100% | 80% |

**Richness preserved** through material quality and micro-detail — not stroke count.

### 5.2 Hero Rule

Illustration window = **visual hero**. Frame side rails combined opacity ≤ **18%** of viewer attention in peripheral vision test.

## 6. Oracle Veil Ring (Inner Energy Border)

**Layer ID:** `L02b_oracle_veil_ring` — inside illustration window, **outside** illustration content.

### 6.1 Intent

Not a second frame. A **magical energy membrane** merging future illustrations with the artifact frame.

### 6.2 Specification

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Offset from velvet inset | 2 px inward |
| Stroke | 0.5 px soft gradient stroke — no hard edge |
| Colors | `#4A2578` (deep purple) → `#7B4BC4` (royal violet) → `#9A7420` (warm gold) at 3 points |
| Opacity | **4–7%** maximum — almost invisible at arm's length |
| Blur | 1 px gaussian equivalent |
| Corner behavior | Softens at corners — energy, not rectangle |

### 6.3 Validation

At 100% zoom, ring is visible. At 50% card width on phone, ring is **felt**, not seen.

## 7. Harmonic Meridian Grid (Corner Connection)

**Problem solved:** Four corners no longer feel independent.

### 7.1 System

Invisible geometric harmony connecting all four corners:

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| **Meridian Threads (OL-6)** | 0.35 px gold lines along hidden octagon connecting corner Anchor Nodes |
| **Corner Anchor Nodes (OL-1)** | 3 px purple crystal at each corner filigree apex — **identical** treatment |
| **Crossing Lines (OL-3)** | At 4 celestial intersection points (mid-edge centers), 6 px gold star burst |
| **Constellation Fragments (OL-5)** | One 3-star fragment midway between each adjacent corner pair, inside filigree band only |

### 7.2 Geometry (Hidden Construction)

Construct regular octagon inset 8 px from outer frame. Corner nodes sit at octagon vertices. Meridian threads follow octagon edges at 12% opacity. **Construction lines not exported** — only energy impression remains.

### 7.3 Subconscious Unity Test

Blur frame to 20 px. Silhouette must read as **one continuous artifact**, not four corner stickers.

## 9. Material Refinement (Premium Pass)

### 9.1 Obsidian

- Depth: 3-stop radial `#030106` → `#060410` → `#0A0812`
- Satin polish: narrow specular band lower-right, 4% white
- No plastic sheen — matte-satin only

### 9.2 Gold

- Hand-polished: micro scratch texture 1% opacity, directional upper-left
- Specular streak: `#F0D77A`, width 1 px, length 8–16 px on filigree curves
- Shadow in grooves: `#9A7420` — always paired with highlight

### 9.3 Crystals

- Facet count: 3–5 visible per crystal element
- Refraction: **soft cyan** `#A8E8FF` at 8–15% on one facet only
- Internal glow: element purple or gold core at 30%
- Never: uniform plastic gradient, neon edge

## 10. Micro Detail Layer (Curiosity Reward)

**Layer ID:** `L01c_micro_engraving`

| Detail | Spec | Visibility |
|--------|------|------------|
| Ancient celestial symbols | Non-alphabetic curves, 0.4 px, gold 12% | Visible at 200%+ zoom |

| Sacred geometry | Hex grid, 2% opacity, full frame | Subconscious |

**Rule:** Micro details never increase macro density. They replace removed side-ornament strokes 1:1 at microscopic scale only.
