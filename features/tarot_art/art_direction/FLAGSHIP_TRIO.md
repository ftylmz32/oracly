# ORACLY Tarot — Flagship Trio Visual Foundation

**Status:** Official reference standard for entire deck  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Cards:** Major 00, 01, 02

These three illustrations define the permanent artistic direction of the ORACLY Tarot Collection. **Every future card must match this trio.**

| Card | ID | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| The Fool | `oracly_tarot_major_00` | Threshold — innocence, destiny |
| The Magician | `oracly_tarot_major_01` | Mastery — will, manifestation |
| The High Priestess | `oracly_tarot_major_02` | Mystery — intuition, inner wisdom |

## Locked Visual Standard (All Three)

### Lighting (identical setup)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Key | Upper-left 45°, warm champagne gold rim |
| Fill | Lower-right, cool royal purple, ~15% |
| Effects | Volumetric god-rays, soft bloom, crystal caustics |
| Shadows | Deep indigo-purple, realistic depth |
| Exposure | Perfect dynamic range — no blowout |

### Color Grading

| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|
| Void ground | `#0B0615`, `#030106` |
| Atmosphere | `#9B6DFF`, `#4A2578` |
| Gold accent | `#D4AF37`, `#F0D77A` |
| Moon silver | `#C0C8D8` |
| Crystal cyan | `#A8E8FF` (facets only) |
| Nebula | `#12071F`, deep midnight blue |

Card 01 may shift **+5% warm gold**. Card 02 may shift **+5% cool silver**. Base grade remains identical.

### Rendering

- Photorealistic fantasy anatomy
- Ultra detailed 8K digital painting
- AAA game concept art finish
- Luxurious fabric with gold embroidery
- Physically believable crystals
- Same particle density (12–18 golden motes)
- Same sacred geometry opacity (~4%)

### Composition

- Portrait vertical (512:896 safe zone)
- Hero centered, strong silhouette
- **8% safe margin** on all edges (frame compositing)
- Illustration only — no text, border, logo
- Minimum 4 depth planes, 15% negative space

### Forbidden

Rider-Waite clones, inconsistent style between trio, cartoon/anime, flat lighting, pure black backgrounds.
