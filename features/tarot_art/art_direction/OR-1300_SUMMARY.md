## Identity Statement

ORACLY Tarot is a **dark-fantasy luxury oracle deck** — cinematic digital painting, volumetric fog, crystal reflections, sacred geometry, gold filigree, deep purple void ground. **100% original.** No Rider-Waite. No third-party tarot art.

## Non-Negotiable Signatures

1. Void ground `#0B0615`–`#030106` (never flat `#000000`)
2. Key light upper-left 45°, warm gold rim
3. Locked luxury gold frame (template — never redrawn per card)
4. OR purple magic emission `#9B6DFF` (soft, volumetric)
5. Crystal facets with internal element-colored glow
6. Sacred geometry ≤4% opacity in backgrounds
7. Minimum 4 depth planes per illustration
8. Minimum 15% intentional negative space
