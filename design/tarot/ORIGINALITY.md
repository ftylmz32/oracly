# ORACLY Tarot — Originality Fence

ORACLY Tarot is an original visual identity. It is not a reskin of any published deck.

---

## Never reference, trace, or “in the style of”

| Tradition | Why it is out |
|-----------|----------------|
| Rider-Waite-Smith / Pamela Colman Smith | Instantly generic “tarot app.” Forbidden compositions, costumes, and props. |
| Crowley–Harris Thoth | Different religion of geometry and color. Not our sanctuary. |
| Tarot de Marseille | Pattern pip cards and woodcut faces are not midnight cinema. |
| Any commercial deck | Including popular modern indie decks. No “inspired by” collage. |

Stock occult packs, “mystic gold frame” marketplaces, and AI prompts that name those decks are forbidden.

---

## Rider-Waite props we never reuse

This list is not complete. If a prop is famous because of Waite-Smith, do not use it.

- Fool: small white dog, white rose, bindle, sun-behind-cliff postcard  
- Magician: garden table with cup / sword / pentacle / wand as a set, infinity hat-brim, red roses / white lilies garden  
- High Priestess: B / J pillars, Torah scroll, lunar crown as costume copy  
- Empress: Venus shield, wheat field postcard  
- Emperor: ram-headed throne  
- Lovers: angel-above-nude-couple mountain  
- Wheel: sphinx + snake + Anubis wheel  
- Moon: crayfish, two towers, barking dog and wolf  
- Sun: child on a white horse, four sunflowers  
- World: dancing figure in a wreath with four evangelist corners  

Marseille pips (decorative club/coin/cup/sword stamps) and Thoth rose-cross / unique Harris palettes are equally closed.

## Prompt and brief rule

Card briefs and generation prompts **must not** contain: Rider-Waite, RWS, Waite, Pamela Colman Smith, Thoth, Crowley, Harris, Marseille, or “classic tarot card of…”.

They **must** contain: ORACLY night, candle key 2900K, antique gold engraving, original motif names, “illustration only — no frame, no text.”

Existing flagship briefs in `features/tarot_art/briefs/` already state “NOT Rider-Waite.” New briefs follow this fence and **this** product palette (`#04030A`, `#E7C56D`, `#4A36B0`), not brighter game purple.

---

## Legal / craft note

Originality is a craft gate, not a feature. If a card would still be recognized as another deck with the title covered, it fails — redraw.
