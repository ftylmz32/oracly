# ORACLY Tarot — Card-Back Principles

The back is the first object of the ritual. It must feel like **one physical artifact** lying on dark cloth.

Do not illustrate a scene on the back. The face holds meaning. The back holds trust.

---

## Purpose

- Identical in a spread (no unique backs)  
- Recognizable as ORACLY at 2 seconds with no logo  
- Calm enough to wait on  
- Original — not a Marseille pattern, not a RWS roses-and-lilies clone, not a Thoth rose-cross  

---

## Field

1. Obsidian velvet `#030106` → `#0A0914` radial, satin, light-absorbing  
2. Laid fiber texture 3% — felt, not a paper photo  
3. Optional Chamber Seven constellation fragment, gold α ≤ 0.12, off-center by 2%  
4. No nebula, no portrait, no large crystal gem  

---

## Frame

Same ceremonial gold language as the face:

- Hairline inner edge α 0.22  
- Engraved outer filigree, aged gold (highlight + groove)  
- **Vesica Compass** at top center (signature, not a logo wordmark)  
- Corner **Anchor Nodes** identical on all four corners  
- Side rails quiet — back is even quieter than the face  
- Oracle Veil Ring 4–7% if needed to marry field to frame  

The back frame is the **same locked template** as the face, not a simpler cousin.

---

## Center emblem (locked)

**Witness Star** — celestial ring (O) + vesica eye + four-point OR light.

Canonical assets: `assets/tarot/logo/` and `assets/tarot/card_back/`.

Scale: emblem ≤ 38% of card height. Readable at thumbnail width.

Threshold Key stays on **face** cards that earn it, not on the deck back.

---

## Symmetry and soul

At arm’s length: four-fold calm symmetry.  
At 200% zoom: 2% micro-imperfection (one fiber catch, one gold scratch) so it does not feel printed by a machine (EPIC-008).

---

## Color mix on the back

Still **70 / 20 / 10**. Gold lives in the line, not in a filled medallion. Burgundy/indigo appear only as velvet undertone, not as a second emblem color.

---

## Motion when faces are down

The back does not animate. If the deck breathes, it is the chamber air — not a pulsing sigil.

Reveal: the pause is on the still back, then the turn. Do not spin, glow-up, or particle-burst the back.

---
