## Signature geometry (always available)

### Midnight Vesica
Two overlapping circles, hairline gold. The lens is empty — a pause, not an eye until we choose the Witness.  
**Use:** Witness Star construction, frame crown, Major threshold cards.  
**Never:** a Christian fish logo, a filled neon almond.

### Celestial Circle
One ring, four tiny meridian ticks (N/E/S/W), open center. Navigation, not a zodiac wheel.  
**Use:** frame, backs, quiet UI marks.  
**Never:** twelve house ticks, astrological glyphs.

### Ancient geometry
Vesica + upward triad + implied octagon meridians. Hidden construction, 8–12% opacity.  
**Never:** Metatron’s cube as a poster, Flower of Life wallpaper.

## Night sky

### Moon phases
Eight engraved crescents. Waxing = increasing gold fill in the crescent; waning = gold leaving. Full moon is a thin ring with a cream disc at 20%, never a yellow cartoon moon.  
**Never:** man-in-the-moon face; RWS moon with crayfish and towers.

### Stars
Four-point and six-point only, unequal sizes, sparse.  
**Never:** five-point pentagram as a “witch” badge; star-trails.

### Constellations
Original **Chamber Seven**: seven points, two bent polylines, slightly imperfect. One fragment per card maximum.  
**Never:** real IAU constellations used as identity (Orion, Scorpius) or copied tarot star cards.

## Human signs (intimate, not occult-generic)

### Threshold Key
Bow is a crescent. Shaft is a single line. Bit is a small void circle (a door, not teeth).  
**Meaning:** entry, pause, permission.  
**Never:** ornate skeleton key clip-art; crossed keys of papal/tarot pope cards.

### Witness Eye
Almond outline. Pupil is a **crescent**, not a full iris. No lashes as decoration.  
**Meaning:** attention, inner seeing.  
**Never:** Hamsa eye; all-seeing pyramid; High Priestess “B/J” pillars with a staring eye.

### Open Palm of Pause
A hand seen almost in profile, fingers slightly apart, as if holding silence. Optional tiny vesica in the palm, 8% gold.  
**Meaning:** offering, halt, care.  
**Never:** hamsa; fortune-teller palm with life-line diagram; pointing “magic” fingers.

## Living quiet

### Night Bloom
Five petals that can be read as a star. Center is a gold seed, not a smiley. Stem is a single engraved curve.  
**Meaning:** opening that takes time.  
**Never:** RWS white rose; lilies of the Magician; lotus sticker.

### Messenger Bird
A long-neck night bird in silhouette (heron-like, original). Wing edge breaks into three tiny stars.  
**Meaning:** a thought arriving.  
**Never:** Egyptian ibis copy; dove-with-olive; crow horror.

### Ribbon of Return
One ribbon, one loop, slightly imperfect infinity. Ends fray into dust, not arrows.  
**Meaning:** coming back to oneself.  
**Never:** DNA helix; festive banner; caduceus.

## Light objects

### Solar Disc of Stillness
Concentric engraved rings, no face, no wings. Inner disc amber at ≤ 12%.  
**Meaning:** presence, noon of the self — not prediction.  
**Never:** winged Egyptian sun; RWS sun with child and sunflowers.
